About us

Hearts for Henna is a service-based organization that promotes henna as a form of art that can unite and benefit various communities through fundraising and charity events. We also seek to teach UF students how to apply henna through workshops.

Fundraisers & charity events

We do henna at campus events, and the proceeds go to our charity of the year.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions where UF students learn how to apply henna.

What is henna?

Henna is a natural dye derived from the dried leaves of the plant Lawsonia inermis. It originated over 5,000 years ago in North Africa and the Middle East.

Henna is often used to decorate the bride's hands and feet for a wedding. A common myth and cultural belief holds that the darker the stain on a bride's hands, the stronger the love between the bride and groom.

Henna is also used as a natural hair dye and provides moisture for the scalp.

We use 100% natural henna at every event and workshop.
